What this prototype does today

  • Full rejections of a single claim, one case at a time.
  • Every regulatory point is tied to a numbered rule with its source.
  • Moratorium, waiting-period and Ombudsman dates are calculated, not guessed.

What it does not do yet

  • Line-by-line analysis of partial deductions.
  • A case queue for a team, or reminders.
  • Ombudsman complaint forms. It has not been scored against real case outcomes.

        IRDAI grievance portal (Bima Bharosa)

        Register at bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in or call 155255. IRDAI forwards the complaint to the insurer and monitors it. There is no claim-size limit.

        C

        Insurance Ombudsman

        Free. File with the office for your area at cioins.co.in. The insurer must comply with an award within 30 days (R10).
